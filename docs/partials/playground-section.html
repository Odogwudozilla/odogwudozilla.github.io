Illustrative simulation · synthetic data · runs entirely in your browser. No requests are sent and no tasks are stored.

Why these responses?
201 · Created
A valid request creates a resource. Its identifier and location are returned.
400 · Bad Request
In this example, a blank title fails validation. Processing is skipped and a field-level error is returned.
503 · Service Unavailable
A temporary service failure creates nothing. A Retry-After header suggests waiting 30 seconds. A real client should consider idempotency before retrying a POST.
