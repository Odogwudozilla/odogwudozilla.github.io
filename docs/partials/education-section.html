04 / The person behind the work

A different path.
A useful perspective.

Before developing financial software, I worked in the operations it supports.

My path took me from economics and banking operations to postgraduate study in information technology and a career in enterprise software engineering. That experience helps me connect technical decisions with business processes and the people using them.

My approach starts with understanding existing behaviour and what users and integrations depend on. I design for compatibility, implement changes incrementally, automate regression checks, and document the contract so the next engineer can follow the reasoning.
